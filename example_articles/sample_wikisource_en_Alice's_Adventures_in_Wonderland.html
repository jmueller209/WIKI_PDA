Alice's Adventures in Wonderland

For works with similar titles, see Alice in Wonderland.
Versions of Alice's Adventures in Wonderland include:
  • Alice's Adventures Under Ground (1864), manuscript illustrated by Lewis Carroll.
  • Alice's Adventures in Wonderland (1866), illustrated by John Tenniel.
  • Alice's Adventures in Wonderland (1901), illustrated by Peter Newell
  • Alice's Adventures in Wonderland (1907), illustrated by Charles Robinson.
  • Alice's Adventures in Wonderland (1907), illustrated by Arthur Rackham

Adaptations

  • Alice in Wonderland (1903), silent film adaptation.
  • Alice in Wonderland in Words of One Syllable (1905), adapted by J. C. Gorham.

Individual poems

  • "All in the Golden Afternoon"
  • "You Are Old, Father William"

See also

  • Through the Looking-Glass, and What Alice Found There (1871), Carroll's sequel.
  • Alice in Blunderland (1907), parody by John Kendrick Bangs.
