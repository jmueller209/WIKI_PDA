Cookbook:Chocolate Chip Cookies

Cookbook | Recipes | Ingredients | Equipment | Techniques | Cookbook Disambiguation Pages | Cuisine of the United States | Dessert

Chocolate chip cookies are cookies with chocolate chips or pieces baked in. Originating in Massachusetts, they are one of the most traditional/popular types of cookies.

Warnings

  • Anyone with peanut or tree nut allergies should be careful when consuming cookies made with nuts or ingredients which may have been in contact with nuts.
  • Consuming raw cookie dough can pose risks due to the uncooked eggs and uncooked wheat flour.
